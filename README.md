# Rollino

**Rollino** is a two-player strategy game combining elements of dice, dominoes, and classic line-making games.

Players take turns rolling a die and placing one of their coloured counters on a matching square. The aim is to be the first player to create a continuous line of **four** counters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Blank spaces can also become **wild squares** when claimed through the surrounding tiles.

Because the board is made from randomly arranged dominoes, every game starts with a different layout.

## The story behind Rollino

Rollino was created by father and son **Richard and Shaun Daubney**.

Richard originally came up with the concept of a tic-tac-toe-style game using a die and a grid of squares representing the different faces of the die.

Shaun suggested replacing the fixed grid with **dominoes**, allowing the layout of the board to change every round. He also introduced the idea of **wild squares**, adding another strategic element to the game.

Together, they developed the idea into a physical wooden prototype before creating this digital version.
